Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026.

J.A.R.V.I.S is a personal assistant used only by its owner. This policy describes how it handles the owner's Google data.

What it accesses

Gmail (read, and send), Google Calendar events (read, create and change) and Google Drive (read, and create new documents), only for the Google account of its owner, who grants that access themselves.

How the data is used

Google data is read only to answer the owner's own requests. To produce an answer, the relevant text is processed on the owner's private server and by the AI model provider the owner has chosen (Anthropic). Sending an email, creating or changing a calendar event, or creating a document happens only after the owner approves that exact action. Nothing is deleted.

What it doesn't do

Google data is never sold, never used for advertising, never shared with anyone else, and never used to train AI models. Data obtained through Google APIs is used in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage

Email and document text that the owner asks about can remain in the owner's own conversation history on their private server; it is not copied anywhere else. The sign-in credential is kept on that server, readable only by its administrator.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
