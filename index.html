J.A.R.V.I.S

J.A.R.V.I.S is a personal assistant built and used by one person, its owner. It is not offered to the public and nobody else can sign in to it.

With the owner's permission it can read their own Gmail, Google Calendar and Google Drive to answer their questions, and, only after the owner approves each action, send an email, add or change a calendar event, or create a Google Doc.
